# 🔇 Fujitsu

This Chrome extension automatically mutes ads on YouTube and Twitch to provide a quieter and less disruptive viewing experience.

---

## ✨ Features

- Mutes audio during video advertisements
- Works on YouTube and Twitch
- Automatically restores sound after ads
- Lightweight and runs in the background
- No user interaction required

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal of this extension is to improve the viewing experience by reducing noise interruptions caused by advertisements on streaming platforms.

---

## 🖥️ How it works

The extension detects when an advertisement is playing and temporarily mutes the tab.  
Once the ad finishes, the audio is restored automatically.
